# Y103 built-in taxonomy

The independent skill ships a compact, reviewable version of the PaperSort-oriented 16-topic taxonomy. It is keyword-assisted, not a scientific ground truth.

1. Host and strain engineering
2. Promoter and transcription regulation
3. Signal peptides and secretion leaders
4. Secretory pathway and protein trafficking
5. Protein folding and endoplasmic-reticulum stress
6. Glycosylation and post-translational modification
7. Protease control and product degradation
8. Metabolic engineering and cofactor balance
9. Fermentation and bioprocess optimization
10. Methanol utilization and alternative carbon sources
11. Recombinant protein production
12. Antibodies, enzymes, and biopharmaceutical products
13. Genome editing and synthetic biology tools
14. Omics, systems biology, and modeling
15. Cell physiology, stress tolerance, and robustness
16. Downstream processing and product quality

The executable keyword definitions live in `scripts/paper_daily.py`. Update this reference and the executable definitions together.
